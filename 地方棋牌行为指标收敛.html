好友房｜快速祈福免费占比

friend_pray_quick_free_ratio · 复合 · 0—1比例

同一窗口内use_quick_pray_lucky_success中temp_c=free的记录数／该事件全部记录数；空值及其他编码不擅自判断为付费。分母为0返回NULL。

展开计算SQL
SELECT DATE(log_date) AS dt, SUM(CASE WHEN temp_c='free' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)*1.0/NULLIF(COUNT(*),0) AS metric_value
FROM friend_ods.behavior_log
WHERE log_date>=DATE_SUB(CURRENT_DATE(),INTERVAL 30 DAY) AND log_date<CURRENT_DATE()
 AND event_id='use_quick_pray_lucky_success'
GROUP BY DATE(log_date);

好友房｜求签免费占比

friend_draw_free_ratio · 复合 · 0—1比例

同一窗口内draw_lots_free_success记录数／（免费成功＋draw_lots_cost_success付费成功记录数）；不计曝光、点击或补签。分母为0返回NULL。

展开计算SQL
SELECT DATE(log_date) AS dt, SUM(CASE WHEN event_id='draw_lots_free_success' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)*1.0/NULLIF(COUNT(*),0) AS metric_value
FROM friend_ods.behavior_log
WHERE log_date>=DATE_SUB(CURRENT_DATE(),INTERVAL 30 DAY) AND log_date<CURRENT_DATE()
 AND event_id IN ('draw_lots_free_success','draw_lots_cost_success')
GROUP BY DATE(log_date);
